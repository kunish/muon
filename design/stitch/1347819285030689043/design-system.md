## Core Direction

- Corporate modern, IDE-like productivity shell.
- Dark mode first, with a compatible light variant.
- High information density with tight 4px-based spacing.
- Three-column model: `64px` global app rail, `240px` navigation pane, fluid workspace.
- Depth comes from tonal layering, not heavy shadows.
